How EGC payment works

After your cylinder request is approved by the Owner, an initial payment of ₹200 becomes available. After the cylinder is delivered, the remaining amount is calculated according to the delivery date and applicable extra charge. Every payment is submitted through the EGC payment process and requires Owner approval.
