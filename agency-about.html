Basic EGC Process

How an emergency request can move through the panel

1
Request Customer submits an emergency cylinder request.
2
Review Agency reviews the request and customer details.
3
Payment Applicable finance/payment information is recorded.
4
Delivery Approved cylinder request moves to delivery.
